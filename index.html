My Personal Pyramid

I wanted to build something I could keep using long after this class: a way to hold myself accountable to the values I'm working toward. This dashboard puts my values at the base of everything, then shows the resources and capabilities I build on them, how I organize my week, and the activities I actually do. It lets me see at a glance where my life lines up with what matters most, and where it doesn't.
